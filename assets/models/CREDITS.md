# Car model

Toy Car by Guido Odendahl, with material extensions and scene composition by Eric Chadwick.

Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ToyCar

The car model is provided under Creative Commons Zero 1.0. The upstream metadata/license document is attributed to KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets and provided under CC BY 4.0 as described in LICENSE.md.

The model is scaled and repositioned for this playground. The glass transmission is reduced for real-time rendering.
